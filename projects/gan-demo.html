Projects / GAN loss demo

Interactive explainer · HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Why GANs use a different loss

A GAN trains two networks against each other. The textbook loss gives the generator almost no signal early in training, exactly when it needs the most help. I built this demo so you can see and feel that problem, then see the fix.

In plain language Picture a student who gets almost no feedback while they are worst at a skill, and lots of feedback only once they are already good. The fix in this demo flips that, so the student gets the strongest feedback early on.

What's mine and what isn't

The non-saturating loss is not my idea. Goodfellow et al. proposed it in the original GAN paper (2014). What I made is this demo and its explanations: the design, the visuals, the simulation and the code.

How it's built

The demo walks through six steps: the two loss formulas side by side, live gradient meters that show the stall, proof that both losses end in the same place, a speed race where each bar moves at the speed of its gradient, a small training simulation, and a free exploration mode.
